YOUR DATA, IN GOOD HANDS

Privacy in
plain words.

Society Synth connects campus society events to the Google Calendar you choose. It uses the information below only to provide that service.

What we use

When you connect Google Calendar, Society Synth receives your Google account ID, email address, and display name so it can recognize your account and show which calendar is connected. It stores the societies you follow and the minimum event-to-calendar IDs needed to update events without creating duplicates.

Google Calendar access

Society Synth requests the Google permission for events on calendars you own. It uses that permission to add and update published society events, and to remove an event it added if the society team cancels it. The app does not read your existing calendar events, invite guests, or share your calendar with anyone.

How information is stored

Your Google refresh token is encrypted with AES-GCM before it is stored in the Society Synth Cloudflare D1 database. Society choices, event records, and sync status are stored there as well. The service uses Cloudflare to host the app and database, and Google to authenticate your account and update Calendar.

Your choices and deletion

You can change the societies you follow at any time. Choose Disconnect calendar in Society Synth to revoke the saved Google connection and remove your account, society choices, and sync records from the service. Events already added to Google Calendar stay in your calendar; you can remove them there at any time.

Event information

Society staff enter event names, dates, times, locations, and descriptions. Published events are shared with students who follow that society and are copied to their Google calendars when a sync runs. Do not add private or sensitive information to event descriptions.

Questions

For privacy questions or account deletion help, contact the Society Synth service administrator through your university's society or student activities team.
